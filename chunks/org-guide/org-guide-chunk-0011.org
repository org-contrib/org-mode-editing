*** Quit and exit
:PROPERTIES:
:UNNUMBERED: notoc
:END:

- {{{kbd(q)}}} (~org-agenda-quit~) ::

  Quit agenda, remove the agenda buffer.

- {{{kbd(x)}}} (~org-agenda-exit~) ::

  Exit agenda, remove the agenda buffer and all buffers loaded by
  Emacs for the compilation of the agenda.
